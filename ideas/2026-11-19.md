1. **ideas: Anthropic gave its Agent Skills standard away**
   - Pick URL: https://www.anthropic.com/news/donating-agent-skills-to-the-agentic-ai-foundation (Anthropic, published 22 October 2026, free to read; source "Anthropic", format "Article")
   - Facts from that page: a skill is a folder with a SKILL.md file that teaches an AI agent how to do a specific task, plus any scripts or reference material it needs. Anthropic has donated the Agent Skills standard, with its name, logo, GitHub organisation and the agentskills.io domain, to the Agentic AI Foundation (AAIF) under the Linux Foundation. Anthropic co-founded the AAIF with Block and OpenAI. More than 70 platforms, including ChatGPT, Cursor and VS Code, use skills. The standard is meant to stay open and vendor-neutral, so the community has a say in where it goes.
   - Angle for the "why": agents are learning everyday know-how the way people do, one skill at a time, and the rulebook for that now belongs to everyone rather than one company. Open standards are how good ideas spread. Keep it factual: no claims about Anthropic's IPO, valuation or models, and nothing beyond what the page says.
